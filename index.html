SPACE​JAM

Blast through 10 sectors. Don't get hit.

How to play

  • Move with WASD / arrow keys (or mouse / touch). Your ship auto-fires forward.
  • Blast enemies streaming in from the right. Clear the wave to face the level boss.
  • Grab power-ups dropped by enemies:
  • W Weapon up H Homing R Rapid S Shield B Bomb L Life
  • Press B (or click) for a smart bomb — clears bullets and hurts everything.
  • Chain kills for a combo bonus. Press P to pause.
